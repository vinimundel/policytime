APPLICABILITY BEFORE EXPLANATION

Follow the decision.

PolicyTime checks when a rule applies before explaining what it says.

01 / YOUR CONTEXT

The expense date is the anchor.

The selected date is when the expense occurred, not when a claim was submitted. Effective start dates are inclusive; end dates are exclusive. Country and worker type determine which clauses apply.

02 / EXPLICIT AUTHORITY

Exceptions have a defined scope.

A contractor limit can replace an employee limit without removing receipt requirements. A newer publication date alone does not establish authority. Unresolved competing rules are shown as a conflict.

03 / INSPECTABLE EVIDENCE

You can open every source.

Answers link to exact passages and the applicable policy version. Quote validation establishes traceability; it does not guarantee that an AI explanation interpreted the passage correctly.

04 / A BOUNDED DEMONSTRATION

Real software. Fictional policies.

The corpus describes Northstar Works, a made-up company. PolicyTime does not approve expenses.
